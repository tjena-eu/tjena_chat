<b>tjena!chat: private messaging from Europe</b>

tjena!chat is a free, open-source messenger based on the open Matrix protocol and FluffyChat. Your chats are end-to-end encrypted, and your account lives on European servers.

<b>Your WhatsApp chats in one place</b>

Run your own WhatsApp bridge with the tjena Bridge app on a spare phone at home. Your WhatsApp messages are decrypted only on that phone and on your devices, and you can call WhatsApp contacts from tjena!chat.

<b>Features</b>

• End-to-end encrypted chats, groups and spaces
• Voice and video calls
• Live location sharing and stories
• Voice messages, stickers and reactions
• Choose when photos and videos download: Wi-Fi, mobile data or Data Saver
• Optional saving of received media to your gallery

<b>Open source</b>

The source code is on GitHub: https://github.com/tjena-eu/tjena_chat
